
# MarketHound Change Log

## v1.2-hf52 — Luna Tactical Discretion / Tools, Not Handcuffs
**Frozen evaluation baseline: 2026-09-04**

- Reframes Luna's tactical mission around positive expectancy and positive realized P&L while preserving capital.
- Preserves the existing technical sensor suite while treating indicators, levels, catalysts, terrain, velocity, phase, and institutional memory as evidence rather than deterministic tactical commands.
- Gives Luna contextual discretion inside deterministic operator ROE.
- Explicitly recognizes `FLAT` as a valid and desirable decision when no favorable asymmetric opportunity exists.
- Establishes that unallocated buying power creates no requirement to trade: **cash is a position**.
- Establishes that the Profit Target is a stopping condition rather than a trading quota.
- Rejects revenge/recovery trading: prior losses do not lower the qualification threshold for the next trade.
- Separates directional correctness from entry quality and location.
- Retains deterministic hard rails including broker truth/routing, dollar exposure, sizing, session profit/loss limits, STOP/FLATTEN, Human Override, short authority, regular-session execution restrictions, and evidence capture.
- Retains premarket/after-hours observation for reconnaissance while regular-session-only broker execution remains enforced.
- Establishes hf52 as the frozen MarketHound evaluation baseline through 2026-09-12.
- During the freeze, daily debug/evidence will be accumulated and reviewed before any strategy modification is authorized.

### Post-freeze research candidates

The following concepts are recorded for evidence-driven evaluation only and are **not implemented in hf52**:

- Local experience archival / "breadcrumbs."
- Similar historical setup retrieval.
- Outcome statistics incorporating sample size, similarity, recency, expectancy, MFE, MAE, and outcome magnitude.
- Experience-informed judgment: measurement -> reasoning -> experience -> judgment.
- Premarket hypothesis formation followed by opening-session price-discovery confirmation.
- Human-style contextual pattern recognition without converting experience into rigid deterministic rules.
